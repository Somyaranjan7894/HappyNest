A note from a friend

💌 Hey, Cutest Girl!

Hey, Rutuprajna! 🥹💗

First of all, I just want to remind you of something: you are genuinely the cutest girl, and your smile deserves to be seen more often! 😂🌷

I know that some days can feel difficult. Sometimes things don't go as planned, our minds get tired, and even small things can feel overwhelming. It's okay to have those days. You don't have to be cheerful all the time, and you don't need to pretend that everything is fine.

Please be a little kinder to yourself. Take things one step at a time, get enough rest, enjoy your favourite songs, and remember that one difficult day doesn't define your whole life. You have your own strengths, and I hope you give yourself the same patience and kindness that you give to other people. 💗

And whenever you need someone to listen, share a silly joke, or simply talk about random things, I'm here as your friend. No pressure to explain everything, no need to reply immediately, and no need to pretend to be okay. Your space and your feelings matter, too. 🫂

May Mahaprabhu Shree Jagannath bless you with peace in your heart, strength for difficult days, wisdom for your decisions, and lots of reasons to smile. 🙏🌼

And finally, one very important reminder: drink some water, take a little break, listen to Bekhudi if you're in the mood, and never underestimate the happiness that a little Dairy Milk Silk or KitKat can bring! 😂🍫

Stay safe, keep growing, and never forget that you deserve kindness and happiness—not just on your best days, but on the difficult ones, too.

With lots of best-friend care,
Your partner-in-crime for silly jokes and chocolate recommendations! 😂💜
