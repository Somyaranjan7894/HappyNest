A prayer for gentle strength

Faith, hope, and better days

A quiet moment of devotion and care.

With reverence and hope

A gentle prayer

May Mahaprabhu Shree Jagannath bless Rutuprajna with peace in her heart, courage through difficult days, wisdom in her decisions, and many reasons to smile.

May her faith bring comfort when life feels uncertain, and may patience remind her that she does not have to solve everything today.

Jai Jagannath.

May hope and kindness stay close, one day at a time.
